# Trademark policy

The code of Pyrsos is free software under the GNU GPL, version 3 or later. You may study it, change
it, and share it, changed or not, under that license.

The name **Pyrsos** and the Pyrsos logo are a different matter. People install this app when their
safety may depend on it, often in a hurry and from whoever offers it. They must be able to tell the
real app from a copy that someone changed, perhaps to spy on them.

So:

1. **Only APKs signed with the project's key are Pyrsos.** The certificate fingerprint is published
   at https://pyrsos.app.
2. **If you distribute a changed version, give it another name and another logo**, and do not
   present it as Pyrsos or as made by the Pyrsos project. Saying that it is *based on* Pyrsos is
   fine.
3. **Unchanged copies of the official APK** may be shared freely, by any means, at no charge, and
   may be called Pyrsos.
4. **Talking about Pyrsos** — articles, reviews, tutorials, research — needs no permission.

Anything else: ask first.
